A one-line voicemail greeting recorded by a cat named Miso — asleep on your clean laundry, promising to wake you at 4 a.m. for food — entered in the [Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443).

`main.py` writes the greeting (116 characters, one line) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
